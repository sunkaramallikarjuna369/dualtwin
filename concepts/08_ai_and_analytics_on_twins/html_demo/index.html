08 - AI and Analytics on Twins

Teaching your twin to think and predict

What is AI & Analytics on Twins?

Simple Explanation

Imagine your twin has a brain that learns from past data. It can spot when something looks "weird" (anomaly detection), predict when a part will fail (predictive maintenance), and suggest the best settings (optimization).

Technical Definition

AI/ML models (time-series forecasting, classification, clustering, reinforcement learning) applied to twin data streams. Includes anomaly detection (isolation forests, autoencoders), predictive maintenance (survival analysis, RUL prediction), and optimization (genetic algorithms, Bayesian optimization).
