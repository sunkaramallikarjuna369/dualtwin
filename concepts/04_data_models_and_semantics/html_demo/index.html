04 - Data Models and Semantics

Giving meaning and structure to your twin's data

What are Data Models & Semantics?

Simple Explanation

Imagine a universal translator for machines. When one system says "temp=72" and another says "temperature_celsius=22", data models help everyone understand they're talking about the same thing - just in different languages!

Technical Definition

Data models define the schema, entities, attributes, and relationships within a digital twin. Semantics add meaning through ontologies (like RDF/OWL), enabling machines to understand context, units, and relationships between data points.

Why Data Models Matter for Twins

🔁

Interoperability

Different systems can share and understand each other's data without custom translations

🔍

Queryability

Ask questions like "Show all pumps with temperature above 80C" across your entire twin

🛠

Maintainability

When you add new equipment, the model tells you exactly what data to capture

🤖

AI-Ready

Machine learning models need consistent, well-labeled data to learn effectively

Where Data Models Are Critical

🏭

Multi-Vendor Factories

Siemens PLCs + ABB robots + custom sensors all speaking different data languages

🏢

Smart Building Portfolios

100 buildings with different BMS vendors need unified energy reporting

🚗

Fleet Management

Trucks from different manufacturers reporting telemetry in different formats

🏥

Healthcare Networks

Medical devices from 50 vendors must share patient data safely
