What is GenAI with Digital Twins?

Simple Explanation

Imagine asking your twin questions in plain English: "Why did Pump 3 fail last Tuesday?" or "Write me a report on energy usage this month." GenAI is the translator that understands your questions and finds answers in the twin's data.

Technical Definition

Large Language Models (LLMs) integrated with digital twin data through RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), function calling, and knowledge graphs. Enables natural language queries, automated report generation, and conversational scenario exploration grounded in twin data.
