What is Business Value & KPIs for Twins?

Simple Explanation

Your CFO asks: "Is this twin worth the money?" Business value is the answer - measured in dollars saved, revenue gained, or risks avoided. KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) are the specific numbers you track to prove it.

Technical Definition

Quantitative and qualitative metrics for measuring digital twin ROI, including Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), Net Present Value (NPV), payback period, OEE improvement, MTBF/MTTR changes, energy savings, and quality yield improvements. Requires baseline measurement and attribution methodology.
