06 - Twin Lifecycle Management

From design to retirement - managing your twin's journey

What is Twin Lifecycle Management?

Simple Explanation

Just like a building has phases - design, construction, operation, renovation, demolition - your digital twin evolves through similar stages. Lifecycle management ensures the twin stays accurate and useful at every phase.

Technical Definition

Twin lifecycle management encompasses version control, configuration management, model updates, data retention policies, and handover processes across design, build, operate, and decommission phases. It ensures twin fidelity and governance throughout the asset's life.

Why Lifecycle Management Matters

🔗

Knowledge Continuity

When engineers leave, the twin preserves their knowledge - no more "tribal knowledge" lost

📄

Version Control

Track every change to the twin model - who changed what, when, and why

🔄

Smooth Handovers

Engineering to Operations handover without losing design intent or context

🗃

Clean Retirement

Archive historical data properly when assets are decommissioned

Where Lifecycle Management is Critical

✈

Long-Life Assets

Aircraft (30+ years), power plants (40+ years), buildings (50+ years) need twins that evolve

🏭

Regulated Industries

Pharma, nuclear, aerospace require full audit trails of all twin changes

🔄

Multi-Phase Projects

Construction projects where design, build, and operate teams are different companies

📊

Fleet Operations

Managing 1000s of similar assets with twins at different lifecycle stages

Who Manages the Twin Lifecycle

📑

Design Engineers

Create initial twin from CAD/BIM models during design phase

🔧

Commissioning Teams

Connect twin to live data and validate against real asset

👷

Operations Teams

Use and maintain the twin during the operate phase

📚

Asset Managers

Decide when to update, upgrade, or retire twins
