What are Industry Use Cases?

Simple Explanation

Every industry has unique challenges. A factory worries about machine breakdowns. A hospital worries about patient flow. A wind farm worries about turbine efficiency. Digital twins solve these specific problems in specific ways.

Technical Definition

Domain-specific implementations of digital twin technology, each with unique data models, integration patterns, analytics requirements, and value propositions tailored to industry-specific challenges and regulatory environments.
