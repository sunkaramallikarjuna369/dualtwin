12 - Governance, Compliance & Standards

Playing by the rules - regulations and standards for twins

What is Twin Governance & Compliance?

Simple Explanation

Your twin must follow rules - just like your business. Governance is who decides what the twin can do. Compliance is proving you follow regulations (like GDPR for data privacy). Standards are agreed ways to build twins so they work together.

Technical Definition

Governance frameworks (data ownership, change management, model validation), regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, IEC 62443, FDA 21 CFR Part 11), and industry standards (ISO 23247, Digital Twin Consortium, DTDL, WoT) that ensure twins are trustworthy, interoperable, and legally compliant.

Why Governance & Compliance Matter

⚖

Legal Protection

Avoid fines (GDPR: up to 4% of global revenue) and lawsuits from non-compliance

🤝

Trust & Credibility

Customers and partners trust twins that follow recognized standards

🔗

Interoperability

Standards let your twin talk to other twins and systems seamlessly

📊

Auditability

Prove to regulators exactly what your twin did and why

Where Compliance is Critical

💊

Pharma & Life Sciences

FDA 21 CFR Part 11 - electronic records must be validated, audit-trailed

🏥

Healthcare

HIPAA - patient data in building twins must be protected

⚡

Critical Infrastructure

NERC CIP, IEC 62443 - power grid twins need industrial security

🌎

EU Operations

GDPR - any twin processing personal data must comply

Who Ensures Compliance
