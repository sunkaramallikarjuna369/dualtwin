03 - IoT and Sensor Data Streams

Real-time data flowing from sensors to your digital twin

What are IoT & Sensor Data Streams?

Simple Explanation

Think of sensors as the eyes, ears, and nose of your digital twin. They constantly watch temperature, listen for vibrations, and smell for gas leaks - then instantly tell the twin what's happening!

Technical Definition

IoT sensor data streams are continuous flows of telemetry data from physical sensors (temperature, pressure, vibration, etc.) transmitted via protocols like MQTT, OPC-UA, or HTTP to digital twin platforms for real-time state synchronization.
