What is a Digital Twin?

Simple Explanation

Imagine having a living copy of a real machine on your computer. When the real machine heats up, your computer copy shows the same temperature. When the real machine vibrates, your copy vibrates too!

Technical Definition

A Digital Twin is a dynamic virtual representation synchronized with a physical entity through continuous bidirectional data exchange, enabling real-time monitoring, simulation, and predictive analytics.
