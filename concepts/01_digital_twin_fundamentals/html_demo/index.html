Understanding the virtual mirror of physical systems

What is a Digital Twin?

Simple Explanation

Imagine having a detailed, living model of your car on your phone. This model knows everything about your actual car - how fast it's going, fuel level, engine temperature, and even when parts might need replacement.

When something changes in your real car, the model updates automatically. This virtual copy is a digital twin.

Technical Definition

A Digital Twin is a dynamic, virtual representation of a physical entity (asset, process, or system) that is synchronized with its real-world counterpart through continuous data exchange.

Key characteristics include bidirectional data flow, real-time synchronization, simulation capability, and lifecycle persistence.
